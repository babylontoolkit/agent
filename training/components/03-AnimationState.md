# TOOLKIT.AnimationState — replaced

`TOOLKIT.AnimationState` was replaced by **`TOOLKIT.Animator`**, a full port of Unity's Animator.
Read [03-Animator.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/babylontoolkit/agent/main/training/components/03-Animator.md) instead —
its section 15 ("Migrating from `TOOLKIT.AnimationState`") maps every old member to the new one. Scenes exported before the change must be re-exported.
